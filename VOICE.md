# Slinky — voice

**Plain, calm and serious, written for everyone.** Slinky holds things people are nervous about losing. The words should feel trustworthy and clear to someone who has never opened Terminal. Updated 2026-10-16 at Derek's direction: no clever labels in the app.

## Rules
- **Say what it is.** Use the names people already know: Keys & Tokens, Recovery (Recovery Codes, Recovery Keys), Projects, Add Item. No invented words ("Kept", "Tuck It Away", "slink").
- **Plain verbs.** Save, Add, Copy, Delete, Lock, Unlock, Import.
- **No wordplay, puns or jokes in the app.** Warmth comes from being clear and kind, not clever.
- **Non-technical first.** Say "variables from a .env file", not "env injection"; "public, not secret", not "plain config". Use technical terms only where a developer needs them (KEY=value, .env, API key), and explain once.
- **Short, complete sentences.** End statements with a period. No exclamation points.
- **Reassure with facts, never fear.** "Saved items are encrypted on this Mac." Not "military-grade" or warnings about hackers.
- **Errors say what happened and what to do.** "Touch ID didn't recognize you. Try again."
- **Buttons name the action.** "Save", "Delete Project", "Print…". Use an ellipsis when another step follows.
