# Third-party material

## Simple Icons
Slinky bundles a small set of service logos from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain). They're in `Slinky/Assets.xcassets/Logos`, generated by `design/services/make_services.py`.

The names and logos belong to their owners. Showing one next to an item only helps you tell your items
apart. It doesn't mean that company made, checked or endorses Slinky.
